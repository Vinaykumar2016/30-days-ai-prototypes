## 🎯 What it Does
Tools like **Lemlist**, **Instantly**, or **Apollo** charge upwards of **₹3,500 to ₹8,500/month** for cold outreach workflows.

This is a **100% free, local B2B Client Acquisition & Pitch Dispatcher**:
1. **Automated Digital Gap Analysis:** Takes any raw lead spreadsheet (like from our **Day 02 Google Maps Scraper**), checks whether the business has a website, evaluates their Google review score, and selects the optimal sales angle:
   - *Missing Website Angle:* High-converting mobile landing page & digital menu pitch.
   - *Sub-4.2 Rating Angle:* Automated Google review recovery & reputation system.
   - *High-Volume Scale Angle:* 24/7 Automated WhatsApp inquiry & appointment booking bot.
2. **1-Click Multi-Channel Dispatch:**
   - Synthesizes bespoke, consultative cold emails (no robotic templates).
   - Generates formatted, direct-to-WhatsApp messages with **1-click clickable `wa.me` links**!
   - Generates clickable `mailto:` links pre-populated with subject lines and bodies.
3. **Master Campaign Spreadsheet:** Exports everything to `outreach_campaign_ready.csv` so you can launch outreach in seconds.

## 🛠️ How to Run Locally (1-Click on Windows)

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Place any `leads.csv` in the folder (or it automatically reads from Day 02!).
3. Double-click **`run_agent.bat`**.  
   *(It automatically parses all businesses and generates `outreach_campaign_ready.csv` in 3 seconds!)*
