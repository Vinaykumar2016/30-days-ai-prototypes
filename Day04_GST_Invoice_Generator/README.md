## 🎯 What it Does
Freelancers and agencies often pay **₹500 to ₹1,500/month** for invoicing apps like Zoho Invoice, Vyapar, or QuickBooks just to send client bills.

This is a **100% free, local, client-side web application** designed specifically for Indian freelancers and tech agencies:
1. **Dynamic GST Auto-Calculation:** Computes CGST + SGST (Intra-state), IGST (Inter-state), or 0% Non-GST automatically in real time.
2. **Instant Scan-to-Pay UPI QR Code:** Generates an authentic NPCI-compliant dynamic UPI QR code embedded directly into the invoice. When clients scan it on Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm, the exact invoice amount and payee name are pre-filled!
3. **Indian Currency Number-to-Words:** Automatically formats the total amount in traditional Indian numbering (`₹21,240` -> *"Twenty One Thousand Two Hundred and Forty Rupees Only"*).
4. **1-Click Pixel-Perfect Print & PDF:** Formats into an executive, clean A4 invoice with full print CSS styling.
5. **Mobile & Tablet Responsive:** Includes instant Edit vs. Preview view toggles on smaller screens.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start
1. Open `index.html` in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Safari).
2. Enter your business details, client details, and invoice items.
3. Click **"Print / Save PDF"** $\to$ choose **"Save as PDF"**, or click **"Share to WhatsApp"** to send the bill instantly!
